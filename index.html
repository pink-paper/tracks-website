Meet the Tonelings

Little movers.
Music makers.

Tonelings play notes as they touch them. You compose by arranging sounds and shaping their paths — a musical idea you can see, hear and keep changing.

  1. 01

    Place your notes

    Choose pitches and instruments. Build a melody or layer several voices.

  2. 02

    Shape their journey

    Use reflectors to change direction. Add split gates, teleports and areas that change their speed.

  3. 03

    Listen. Tinker. Repeat.

    Press play, try a different arrangement, and save your track when it clicks.
